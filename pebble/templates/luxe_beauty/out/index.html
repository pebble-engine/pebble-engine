The atelier

A studio in SoHo, a story in every bottle

made by hand

Every Maison Lume formulation begins on a quiet bench in our SoHo atelier — botanicals weighed by the gram, tested across textures and tones, refined until they belong on a shelf you reach for first. We name nothing finished until the people who will wear it tell us it feels like theirs.

The studio is open to visitors by appointment. Bring questions, your routine, or a friend; leave with a recommendation written in our own hand.
