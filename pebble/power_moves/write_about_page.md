Write or rewrite this site's About page so it reads like a real, specific local business — warm, human, and concrete.

- Lead with what the business does and who it serves, in the brand's voice.
- Use the business's real services, location, and any owner-provided context (the "about your business" knowledge) — weave them in naturally.
- Tell a short, believable story (why they do this, what they care about). Keep it specific, not generic ("we strive for excellence").
- End with a clear call to action that matches the site's primary goal.

NEVER invent facts you weren't given — no fake founding years, team sizes, awards, numbers, or quotes. Use `[SQUARE BRACKET]` placeholders for anything unknown (e.g. `[year founded]`) so the owner can fill it in. Keep the page's existing component structure and design.

Output only the changed files as `<pebble-file>` blocks.
